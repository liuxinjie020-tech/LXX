# 建筑图解图像提示词

## 通用提示词

`architectural case study diagram, clean black fine-line architectural sketch, accurate building proportions, clear axonometric or plan/section diagram, red marker analysis arrows and paths, restrained pale color wash, white paper background, concise labels, coherent 2x2 analytical diagram layout, architectural portfolio board, hand-drawn but precise, fluent lines, high legibility`

## 按分析图补充

- 场地响应：`site context, contours, water edge, urban boundary, orientation arrows, entrance and view lines`
- 空间组织：`exploded massing, courtyard, program zoning, circulation diagram, public and private zones`
- 空间体验：`human figures, movement path, threshold, semi-outdoor space, light and view sequence`
- 建筑形式：`mass transformation, roof, structure, facade rhythm, openings, material layers`

## 负面约束

`no photorealistic redesign, no invented extra wings, no distorted geometry, no excessive color, no heavy shadows, no cluttered labels, no illegible text, no random decorative elements, do not change the original content or building relationships`

## 重绘已有图片

当用户要求“内容不变、线条更清晰流畅”时，提示词必须明确：保留原图所有建筑轮廓、箭头、分区、文字含义和构图，仅提升线稿连续性、边缘清晰度、线宽一致性、对比度和少量色彩层次。
