# Handbook PDF quality guide

## Content density

- Use empty space deliberately for covers, section openers, and short self-test pages. On knowledge pages, add a relevant map, plan, structure drawing, comparison strip, or concise recall box before leaving a large unused area.
- Prefer one strong, legible figure to several tiny diagrams. Use two related figures only when they teach a useful comparison.
- Keep a figure with the heading and bullets it supports. If the whole group cannot fit, move the group to the next page.

## Diagram selection and attribution

- Start with the supplied reference library. Crop to the plan, section, detail, or object that is actually discussed.
- For external images, prefer official cultural institutions, government, museums, universities, academic publishers, or reusable archives. Do not use an image solely because it is visually attractive.
- Maintain a figure ledger with: handbook section, figure title, source type, exact book/PDF page or URL, and a one-line description of what it helps the learner identify.
- Use neutral captions such as "看图提示：先找……，再观察……". Distinguish archaeological evidence, reconstruction, and schematic interpretation.

## A5 layout rules

- Default to A5 portrait for technical Chinese study handbooks. Use a quiet white background, compact header/footer, and a logo that is contained rather than stretched.
- Put major sections on a clean new page when that improves scanability. Numbered knowledge points should be visually heavier than body text and use the accent color sparingly.
- Keep body leading compact but readable. Make important terms bold and/or accent-colored; do not use emphasis for entire paragraphs.
- Avoid ornamental boxes before every bullet. Use a simple dash or a light hierarchy instead.
- Choose contain-fit for plans and labeled drawings; crop photographs and scanned pages enough to remove irrelevant margins while preserving the subject.

## Final checklist

- The teaching order follows the recording.
- Claims, names, dates, and diagrams are traceable to a supplied source or a recorded public source.
- Every visual is relevant to the nearby point and has an exact caption.
- No title, numbered point, visual-caption pair, or table is split across pages.
- No logo is distorted; page colors are consistent; headings are visibly stronger than the body.
- The latest PDF has been rendered and every page visually inspected.
- The final PDF reopens successfully and is saved to the exact location requested by the user.
